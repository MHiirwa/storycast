About & Access

Our mission

StoryCast shares audio and video stories in a way that works for everyone. Good storytelling shouldn't depend on how you see, hear, or move through the web.

Accessibility features

  • Captions & transcripts

    Every video has captions, and every story has a full text transcript.

  • Keyboard friendly

    Use Tab to move around. A skip link jumps straight to the content, and focus is always visible.

  • Readable colour & type

    Text meets WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, and sizes scale with your browser settings.

  • Screen reader support

    Semantic landmarks and labelled navigation help assistive technology describe each page.

How to engage

  1. Choose a story from the home page.
  2. Watch or listen, with captions on if you like.
  3. Open the transcript to read along or revisit a moment.
